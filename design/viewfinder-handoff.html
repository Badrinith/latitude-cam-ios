Every control, and where it went

The handoff's viewfinder has five dials, a film carousel and a three-item bottom bar. The app has more than that. Nothing was cut to make the two agree — each existing control took a place in the new language instead.

Control todayWhere it landsWhy there
Shutter, ISO, White Balance, ExposureDIALFour of the five knurled dials on the top plateExactly the handoff's spec. Each becomes a real rotary control, not a sheet launcher — the sheet stays as the expanded view.
Aperture WAS BURIEDDIALFifth dial, far left, f/2.8The handoff gives aperture its own dial. It exists in AppState already but had no face on the viewfinder — this is a gain, not a port.
PRO toggleBARLeft slot of the bottom barThe handoff parks a "VIDEO" placeholder left of the shutter. PRO is the real thing that belongs in a permanent slot.
Pro barrel clusterFEEDReplaces the film carousel while PRO is litBoth want the same band above the bar, and they are never wanted at once — one is framing, the other is setting up.
Film selector (two-tier)FEEDFilm-stock card carouselThe handoff's replacement for the flat strip. Swipes between stocks; tap opens Film Sim.
Lens selectorFEEDGlass segmented row above the barReal lenses outrank the "VIDEO" placeholder they displace, and the glass pill is the handoff's own HUD material.
Flip camera · Portrait · SettingsPLATEUtility row in the plate's 44px headThe handoff leaves that band empty above the dials. A metal top plate is exactly where body switches live on a real camera.
Aspect ratioHUDThe "RAW · 3:2" pillAlready in the handoff and already shows the aspect. Tap cycles, press-and-hold opens Settings.
Grid / compositionPLATEUtility rowA view aid, grouped with the other view aids rather than with exposure.
HistogramHUD96×40 glass panel, top-leftSpecified in the handoff, unchanged.
Meter readout · Status pill · ZoomHUDGlass chip column under the histogramThe handoff defines the glass pill as its HUD chip. These are three more of them, stacked so the frame's centre stays clear.
Gallery thumbnailBARRight of the shutter, 26×26Handoff spec, unchanged.
ReleaseBAR62px ring + 50px fill, centreHandoff spec. Still the one thing that never moves.
Tap-to-meter · Pinch-to-zoom · RAW progress · Save toastFEEDUnchanged over the feedBehaviours, not chrome — the redesign does not touch them.
Control style (Classic · Bellows · Crown)SETTINGSJoined by a fourth: "Top Plate"This becomes another selectable deck, so nothing you already had stops working.
Two deliberate departures from the handoff, both because the app is real and the reference is not: the "VIDEO" placeholder becomes the lens selector, and the empty band above the dials becomes the body switches. Everything else follows the tokens as written.
