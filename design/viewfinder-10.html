Ten ways to put the controls away

A camera should show you the picture, not the software. Each concept below hides every setting until it is asked for, and brings it back as something with knurling on it — a knob, a collar, a lever — that you turn rather than tap. Drag any knob to feel it.

Shared across all ten: the release never moves, the frame lines never rotate,
and nothing that isn't a picture survives more than three seconds of not being touched.
