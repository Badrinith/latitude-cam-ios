Latitude Cam / Pro controls, proposed / 4 Aug 2026

The dial belongs in the frame, not on top of it.

Today the Pro sheet slides up and covers the picture, so every adjustment is made blind — you change a stop, dismiss the sheet, then find out what it did. This proposal removes the sheet. Tapping a readout blooms that one control into the middle of the viewfinder as a large translucent dial: the scene stays visible through it, and the exposure changes live underneath your thumb while you turn.

One control at a time, at thumb scale

Tap 1/125 in the readout strip and the shutter dial blooms. The strip segment stays amber so it is obvious which control is live.

Index at twelve

The amber mark sits outside the ring, exactly as it does on the existing top-plate dials. Values turn under it; the mark never moves.

Clear centre

Only the rim is frosted. The middle stays transparent, so the subject is never behind glass — the point of putting the control here at all.

Live underneath

Exposure applies as you turn, not on release. The frame you are looking at is the frame you will get.

Drag anywhere

The whole viewfinder is the gesture surface while a dial is open — not a 132pt circle. This is the fix for the dials that will not turn today: there is no small target left to miss.

A click per stop

One detent haptic per value, at the moment the value changes. Two soft ticks at either end of the scale, so you feel the limit instead of wondering.

Gets out of the way

Closes on a tap, on the shutter, or on its own after four seconds idle — the way a meter times out. Never a mode you can get stuck in.

The four states

Idle, a turning dial, the auto position, and the mode plates that replace the format and resolution chips.

Decisions I need from you

Each of these changes what gets built. I have given my recommendation, but they are yours to make.

01

Does the bottom sheet go entirely?

This proposal deletes it. Everything that lived there becomes either a bloom dial or a mode plate. That also removes the Reset / Undo / Redo row — I would move those to Settings rather than lose them. Recommended: yes, delete it.

02

Exposure compensation: fourth dial, or a strip?

EV is the one control you often want visible while framing rather than opened. It could live as a permanent thin scale along the bottom edge instead of a dial you have to summon. Recommended: keep it a dial for now, revisit once the dials feel right.

03

Vertical drag as a second axis?

A real camera has two command dials. Horizontal could turn the open dial while vertical trims exposure at the same time. Powerful, and easy to trigger by accident. Recommended: no, not in the first pass.

04

Should the film ring bloom the same way?

The knob at the bottom already turns through the four stocks. Making it use the same bloom would be consistent, but it is the one control that genuinely wants its thumbnails visible. Recommended: leave it alone.

Rendered with the app's own tokens from Theme.swift — Ink, Tone, Accent, the film swatches, SF Pro and SF Mono in their existing roles — so this is a proposal about behaviour and layout, not a repaint. The scene behind the glass is drawn, not photographed; it is there so the transparency can be judged. Nothing here is built yet.
