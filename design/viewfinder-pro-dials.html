Dials that hide, barrels that answer, film you swipe

PRO no longer lives at the bottom — it opens and closes the plate itself. Turning a dial drops a barrel under it for the fine adjustment. The film carousel is glass now, swipes with detents, and moves to the ground-facing edge when the body turns.

1 · The plate opens and closes

PRO is gone from the bottom bar. It is a switch on the plate, and what it does is reveal the dials — so the same tap that says "I am setting up" is the tap that puts the instruments on screen. Closed, the plate is a thin strip and the picture gets the rest.

2 · Turn a dial, get a barrel

A 44pt dial is a coarse control — good for grabbing, poor for landing on 1/250 exactly. So the moment a dial moves, a barrel slides out beneath the plate with real travel and detents, reading the value under a fixed amber index. It retires a second after you let go. Drag any dial below to see it.

3 · Landscape

Turn the body and the barrels come to the top — under the plate, where the fingers of the supporting hand already are — while the film carousel drops to the bottom edge. The release never moves.

4 · The film carousel

Translucent rather than a solid gold card: it sits over the picture, so it should let the picture through. Swipe left and right; every stock crossed is a detent with its own tick of haptic feedback.
