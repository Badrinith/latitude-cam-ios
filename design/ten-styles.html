Latitude Cam / Ten directions / Pick one

Ten ways to put the film below the shutter.

Two rules hold across all ten. The stock selector sits under the shutter button where it cannot cover the picture — the film knob's 340pt arc is gone. And the exposure controls live as small readouts in the finder, opening into a translucent dial only while a thumb is on them. What differs is the material: film rebate, lens barrel, meter needle, counter window. Pick the one that feels like the camera you want this to be.

If you want my read

You asked for options, not an opinion, so take these or leave them.

07

Thumbwheel is the one I would build

It is the only direction whose appearance tells you the gesture: a knurled wheel asks to be dragged sideways, which is exactly the gesture. That matters more than usual here, because the control it replaces failed for reasons nobody could see.

03

Contact tiles if the film looks are the product

It is the only one that shows what a stock will actually do to the scene in front of you. The cost is four extra renders — but that machinery already exists and runs at 4Hz for the current knob, so it is nearly free.

01

Rebate if you want it to feel like film first

The most on-brand of the ten and the closest to the app's existing voice. Weakest at answering "what will this look like", since it gives you names rather than colour.

05

Cartridges is the one I would avoid

Charming for a week. Skeuomorphism that does no work tends to curdle, and canisters do not tell you anything about the picture.

Two things to decide with it

A

Settings gets full-size knobs regardless

You asked for physical knob controls in Settings as well as small ones in the finder. That is the same component at two sizes, so whichever direction you pick, Settings gets the large version with room to breathe and the finder gets the compact one. No extra choice needed.

B

Does the compact row show three readouts or four?

Shutter, ISO and white balance fit comfortably. Adding exposure compensation makes four and starts crowding the top edge on a small phone. My inclination is three in the finder, with EV reachable by a swipe up from the shutter.

All ten use the app's own tokens — Ink, Tone, Accent, the four film swatches, SF Pro and SF Mono in their existing roles — so these differ in material and gesture, not in palette. The scene behind the glass is drawn rather than photographed; it is there so the transparency can be judged. Nothing here is built yet: tell me a number and I will build that one.
