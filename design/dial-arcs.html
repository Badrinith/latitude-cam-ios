A dial set into the body, showing only its crown.

A large wheel whose centre is below the screen: the scale enters at the bottom left, climbs the arc, crests under a fixed index, and leaves at the bottom right. Values ride the rim — each is placed at the top and rotated about a point below, so it swings down the curve the way engraving on a real dial does. Radius is the whole variable: a tight wheel shows three values steeply, a wide one shows seven in a gentle bow.

If you want my read

01

One wheel, four radii, is what I would build

Standard, Wide, Tight and Micro are not four components — they are one wheel given a different radius. Radius is the only thing that has to change between the film selector, the pro cluster and the editor's rows, and building it once means every control in the app stays the same object at four sizes.

02

Upright type is the decision to make first

Engraved type is what makes it read as a real dial, and it is also what makes VERMILION lie on its side at the edge of the arc. Numbers survive the rotation; long names do not. My inclination is engraved for the pro controls, upright for the film selector — but that is a split worth seeing on device before committing to it.

05

Ticks earn their place

Between two stops the numbers barely move, so without graduations the wheel looks static until it snaps. The ticks are what make the turn visible, and they cost nothing but a mask.

06

Twin wheels are handsome and the tallest thing here

Concentric rims are exactly how a shutter dial carries an ISO collar, and it maps onto the two-tier selector that already ships. At 132pt it also takes more of the frame than anything else on this page, directly above the release.

Each wheel is a circle whose centre sits below the screen; values are placed at the crown and rotated about a point at the radius, so they travel the arc rather than being drawn along one. The release is at scale in every frame so what each covers can be judged. Nothing is built — give me a number, and say whether the type should turn with the wheel or stay upright.
