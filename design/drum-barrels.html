Latitude Cam / Drum barrels / Ten to pick from

Half a cylinder, turning on a real axis.

The barrel today is a flat strip with its ends darkened — curvature implied by shading, which is why it reads as a list that has been dimmed rather than as something round. These ten put the values on the surface of an actual cylinder: each one is rotated about the drum's axis and pushed out to its radius, so the ones at the edge are foreshortened because they are turned away from you, not because they have been faded. Radius is the variable that matters — a tight drum shows three values steeply, a wide one shows six almost flat.

01

One drum, three radii, is what I would build

These are not ten components. Standard, Deep and Micro are the same drum at three radii, and radius is the only thing that needs to differ between the film selector, the pro cluster and the editor's rows. Building one and passing it a number is a smaller change than it looks, and it keeps every control in the app the same object at three sizes.

03

Half dome if height is the constraint

It is the most literal answer to what you asked for — half the cylinder, crown up — and it costs about half the height of a full drum at the same radius. That matters most in the film selector, which sits below the shutter where there is least room.

07

Glass is the one to try in the viewfinder

The only direction that does not cover the picture it sits over. Its weakness is that legibility then depends on what you are pointing at, which is exactly the wrong dependency for a control — worth a look on device before trusting it.

08

Vertical solves a problem the editor actually has

Horizontal barrels inside a vertical scroll are why the panel would not scroll — the barrels took every drag. Turning them ninety degrees hands the vertical axis back to the scroll and needs no axis rule at all. The cost is that only one control is visible at a time.

Each drum is real 3D: every value is rotated about the cylinder's axis and pushed out to its radius, so the ones at the edge are foreshortened because they are turned away rather than because they have been dimmed. That is the difference from the barrel shipping today, which darkens its ends to imply a curve it does not have. Nothing here is built — give me a number, and say whether the same drum should serve all three screens.
