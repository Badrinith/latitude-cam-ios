Machined rings — before I build it

Same geometry you have now: two semicircles struck from the shutter, Level 1 naming the setting and Level 2 its values. What changes is only how they are made — the reference's milled rim, copper hairline, engraved ticks and centre readout, applied to the arcs rather than to floating round dials.

WHAT I AM TAKING FROM THE REFERENCE

A milled rim. Fine dot knurling across the band, not the flat stripes there now.

A copper hairline. One warm line along each edge of the ring — the single detail that makes the reference read as machined rather than drawn.

Engraved ticks. A cut mark between values, so the scale reads as continuous metal instead of spaced words.

A centre readout. The reference states the setting, its value and its auto state as a stacked block. Ours goes in the well between shutter and inner ring, where PRO already is — so PRO becomes the readout, and reset sits beside it.

Not taking: full circles. The reference's dials are round because they sit on a panel with room. Ours circle the shutter, which is what makes them thumb-reachable and edge-to-edge.
